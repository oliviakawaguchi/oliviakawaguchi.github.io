How it ranks

  • Change. For revenue growth, operating margin, gross margin, inventory/revenue, contract assets/revenue and asset turnover, the latest year-on-year move is scored against the company's own previous twelve, using median and MAD so one odd quarter can't set the yardstick. A move counts when |z| ≥ 2 and it is economically large. Direction is recorded, not scored.
  • Price. EV/EBITDA on trailing twelve months (GAAP operating income plus D&A) against the median of at least five same-industry peers in the universe (SIC code).
  • Rank. The average of the two percentile ranks, among companies with a material change and a usable EV/EBITDA.
  • Guards. A company that pays interest but shows no readable debt is left out of the price test rather than called cheap. A margin move with |z| ≥ 10 is treated as a likely one-off and listed under “Read the filing first”.
  • No look-ahead. Each past day only uses filings that had been filed by that day.
  • What it is not. The original funnel's last steps (reading every filing, hand-built peer groups, a +30% return hurdle on consensus estimates) are not automated here. This is the front of the funnel, running live.

Sources: SEC XBRL company facts; daily closes from Yahoo Finance. Debt and EBITDA are read from tagged filings and are approximate.
