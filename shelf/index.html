What this does not say

floorsCounts stop at the first results page, about 45 products. Harmons reads 43 Monster and 44 Red Bull; both are floors, not counts.
not facingsDistinct products, not shelf space and not volume. A chain can carry twelve flavours in one narrow bay.
grocery onlyAll 25 are supermarket banners, Meijer the one supercenter. Convenience — where energy actually sells — is absent entirely, along with mass, club, drug and fountain. Every convenience chain we tried refuses to show a catalogue without a named store. That gap runs one way: it understates Black Rifle, which is built for convenience, and leaves grocery-led Bloom close to whole. Keurig Dr Pepper’s 799 is the conservative side of the error.
a feed gapRed Bull reads zero at Smith’s, King Soopers and Fred Meyer, on fully loaded pages. Three Kroger divisions, one answer: the brand is almost certainly missing from Kroger’s delivery feed rather than from the shelf. Red Bull’s total is understated.
remeasuredAn earlier read of this same shelf used page text and missed products that load late. Nine of a hundred cells read empty when they were not, and two findings drawn from them were withdrawn. These counts replace them.
