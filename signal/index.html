Tools show up at customs about four months after the building is done

HS 8486 is the customs code for chip-making tools. We measured the lag once, at a fab we can see finish, and use it as a ruler for the fabs still being built.

Kyushu tool imports vs JASM fab 1

HS 8486 imports cleared at Moji Customs (Kyushu, incl. Fukuoka Airport), JPY bn per month. Shaded: JASM exterior finished, seen by satellite.

Exterior done Feb to May 2023. Imports' 3-month average passes 2x the 2022 level in Sep 2023 and peaks in Nov 2023 at ¥28.4bn. That gap, about 4 months, is our ruler: tools go in ≈ building done + 4 months.

One fab, three clocks: JASM fab 1

The same fab seen three ways: the building from orbit, MKS's revenue from Japan (10-Q), and tools arriving at Kyushu customs. Tap a bar for the number.

The order holds. Building shell done Feb to May 2023 → tool imports jump Sep to Dec 2023 → fab opens Feb 2024 → mass production Dec 2024.

MKS Japan held up while the cycle fell. In 2023 MKS semiconductor revenue fell 28%, but its Japan revenue rose 6% to $280m. Japan's share of MKS revenue rose from 7.4% to 7.7%, and was 8.1% in 4Q23.

Quarter by quarter, it does not line up. MKS Japan revenue and Kyushu imports show no correlation at a 0 to 2 quarter lead (about −0.2 to 0). MKS books revenue where its customer, the toolmaker, takes delivery, not at the fab. Japan also includes chemistry, and a ransomware attack pushed 1Q23 shipments into 2Q23.

Now: MKS Japan revenue is rising again, $91m in 3Q26 (+62% y/y), while JASM fab 2's frame goes up. Worth watching, not yet proof.

Sources: MKS 10-Qs and 10-Ks, geographic revenue by shipped-to location (recast basis from 2Q24; 2023 quarters from 2024 10-Q comparatives; 1Q and 4Q derived from year-to-date totals). Japan Customs via e-Stat. Copernicus Sentinel-2.

Sources: Japan Customs via e-Stat (JPY, Sep 2026 provisional); UN Comtrade public API (USD). Taiwan is not a Comtrade reporter, so its line is exports to Taiwan reported by the US, Netherlands, Japan, Korea, Singapore and Germany. Korea's 2026 months are not yet published.

Board makers lead MKS chemistry by a quarter or two

MKS sells plating chemistry to the makers of AI server boards and substrates. Five Taiwan board makers report revenue every month, so we see their volume long before MKS reports.

Taiwan board makers vs MKS chemistry, year-on-year growth

Bars: Unimicron, Zhen Ding, Kinsus, Nan Ya PCB and Tripod combined revenue (MOPS). Dots: MKS E&P chemistry, excluding FX and palladium (earnings calls).

Taiwan 5 board makersMKS chemistry

Correlation over 11 quarters: 0.69. Chemistry is smoother (std dev 9.7 pts vs 12.4). Board makers grew +32% in 2Q26 and +46.5% in 3Q26, the fastest in the series. That points to strong chemistry in 2H26.
